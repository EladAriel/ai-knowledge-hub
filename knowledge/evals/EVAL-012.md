# EVAL-012: MASIF: successful agent runs hide failures

## Summary

1,828 multi-agent traces: hidden failures in 54.3% of successful traces and 75.2% of failed ones.

## Details

Watching only outcomes misses more than half the process problems (LLM-labeled with human spot checks). Keep traces of every step; report a hidden-failure rate next to task success; ….
