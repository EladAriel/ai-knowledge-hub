## Summary

19 models, 41 tasks on simulated Salesforce/Zendesk/Jira/Slack: 40% of runs broke a rule.

## Details

Top failures: false claims about actions taken or record contents (290 runs), following instructions planted in content (266). Vendor paper (Era by Eon). Motivates authority tiers per source.
