# EVAL-008: FDE-Gym: The Last Mile of AI Delivery

## Summary

Benchmark of 95 FDE tasks from 20 customer scenarios in 18 sectors.

## Content

### 1. [FDE-Gym: The Last Mile of AI Delivery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22350)
[Code](https://github.com/deeplethe/fde-gym) · [Website](https://fde-gym.com)
- **What it is:** A benchmark of 95 "delivery" tasks (FDE)  works with virtual stakeholders and systems that are similar to production. The grade is the improvement in the client's business index minus the cost of referrals, and not just if the test is passed.
- **Why new:** The client's request can target the wrong problem, and the agent has to discover unspoken constraints on his own. This is an evaluation axis that enterprise AI products lack.
- **Evidence:**
  - 13 model configurations from 7 suppliers were tested.
  - At the most realistic level, the leading model reached a score of about 0.56 and completed only 14 out of 95 tasks.
  - The main bottleneck is the discovery of the constraints.
  - The harness is open, but most tasks are only available upon request.
- **Scores:** Effectiveness 5/5, hype 3/5.
- **Fit:** High. **medium to high** effort.
- **Recommendation: Adopt** as an evaluation axis and as a regression test for organizational agents.
