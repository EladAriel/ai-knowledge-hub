# EVAL-006: Jev Meets the Trolley Problem

## Summary

5,240 scenarios on 14 decision models, including Jev and GPT-6 Luna.

## Content

**5. [Jev Meets the Trolley Problem](https://zachlandes.com/trolley-problem/)** (Zach Landes)
- **What it is:** 5,240 scenarios on 14 decision models, including Jev and GPT-6 Luna. The test is about consistency, not morality.
- **Why new:** This is the first independent test, i.e. not of a doubt, of some decision models that are sensitive to formulation and order.
- **Evidence:**
  - Changing the order of description alone reversed Jev's decision in 370 out of 2,620 pairs.
  - In 942 cases he answered "yes" to both the question and its opposite, or "no" to both.
  - GPT-6 Luna was the most consistent, and still had 744 inconsistencies.
  - The analysis plan is predetermined, and the author indicates the limitations.
- **Scores:** Effectiveness 3/5, Hype 2/5.
- **Adaptation:** Those who use decision models for routing or as a gate need an eval that replaces the order of options, changes the wording and asks the opposite question.
- **Effort:** Low.
- **Recommendation: Watch**, and adopt the consistency tests already now. This is the fifth time that Jev has appeared since 10.10.
