# METH-191: Scribe: more context made the agent worse

## Summary

"Context layer" products keep piling more docs into the agent's context. Notion, Box and Snowflake all launched something like this in early November. Scribe ran 30 business tasks 4 ways. A wiki alone and recordings of how people actually did the work each helped by more than half. Recordings beat the wiki on every measure. Wiki plus recordings was worse than recordings alone. This is a vendor test (Scribe sells workflow recordings), on one model, graded by that same model. Treat it as a hint, not proof. Why it matters: docs that look relevant but don't answer the question can hurt answers. Retrieve less, and prefer traces of how the work was actually done. For: product and architects.
