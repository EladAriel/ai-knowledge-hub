# METH-217: New terms (Nov 25, methods): Accountable Agents Framework, Taxi, ContextLure

## Summary

One-line mentions from the Nov 25 methods digest that did not become a full item (no full evaluations).

## Details

Accountable Agents Framework (AAF): a CAIS proposal for private, unlinkable identity certificates for agents, each tied to a responsible party ([paper](https://www.accountableagents.ai/paper.pdf)).

Taxi: a system for analyzing millions of agent steps with clustering and SQL, including decision models in the style of Jev ([arXiv 2611.31457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31457)). Decision models have come up before.

ContextLure: a prompt injection that exploits an innocent chat history, with 67% success on AgentDojo ([arXiv 2611.31424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31424)).

Loop Transformer in GLM-5.4, named as a term yesterday, got a short update on METH-212 (not a new row).

Filtered and therefore not added: a forecast that "80% of junior jobs" will go, and debates about banning open models, because those are opinions with no data; and a study of agent shopping that arrived from a news summary with no primary source.
