# METH-143: New terms (Nov 12): FERN, Recursive Capability Composition, PARLEY / PAC, Nod, United Airlines agent booking ban

## Summary

One-line mentions from the Nov 12 methods digest (no full evaluations). PAF, Meta's Muse Ember, and Gemini 4 Carbon with /swarm in Antigravity are repeats from Nov 11 and are not listed again.

## Details

FERN (arXiv 2611.14477): table retrieval for text-to-SQL without an LLM, with three retrieval components and LightGBM, in 208 milliseconds. Recursive Capability Composition (arXiv 2611.13196): BPMN models that become agentic workflows and are exposed as tools to other agents. PARLEY / PAC taxonomy (arXiv 2611.14242): a taxonomy of failures of collaboration between agents, in perception, adaptation and coordination. Nod (n8n Labs): a personal open-source agent that runs locally and asks for approval before every sensitive action. United Airlines forbids AI agents from booking flights (528 points on HN): an early sign of a commercial block on buying through agents. Repeats from yesterday, not listed again: PAF (arXiv 2611.13055), Meta's Muse Ember, and Gemini 4 Carbon with the /swarm command in Antigravity.
