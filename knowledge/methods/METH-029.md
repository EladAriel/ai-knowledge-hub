# METH-029: The hidden instructions for LLM in the WhatsApp help pages

## Summary

In 80 out of 545 help pages, 102 blocks of hidden text were found, written to the support chatbot.

## Content

**2. [The hidden instructions for LLM in the WhatsApp help pages](https://redact.dev/blog/whatsapp-help-pages-hidden-ai-instructions)** (Redact, 306 points on HN, and verified by WIRED against WhatsApp)
- **What it is:** In 80 out of 545 help pages, 102 blocks of hidden text were found, written to the support chatbot. Among other things, they ordered him not to recommend contacting the data protection regulator, and not to cite GDPR.
- **Why new:** This is a real case where a public knowledge base is also used as an instruction channel for the bot.
