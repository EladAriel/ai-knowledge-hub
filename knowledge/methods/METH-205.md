# METH-205: New terms (Nov 23, methods): HookEvo, ToRA, FrameCheck, 0rwx

## Summary

One-line mentions from the Nov 23 methods digest (no full evaluations).

## Details

HookEvo: "trains" the harness through the runtime's hooks. It continues GEPA from November 21 (METH-196) and skill evolution from November 22 (METH-198) ([arXiv 2611.27294](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27294)).

ToRA: a guard model that runs as an extra line in the same decode, adding less than 4% to latency ([arXiv 2611.26975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26975)).

FrameCheck: removes from reports the LLM wrote any interpretation that was not in the input ([code](https://github.com/seunghan96/framecheck)).

0rwx: a Unix-style permission scale for describing how much AI did in a piece of work ([site](https://0rwx.com)).

Filtered and therefore not added: OpenAI's "Perplexity and Intelligence," because it is an opinion and policy paper, not a method; and posts on X about context engineering that were mainly promotion for books and courses. Nov 24 update: FrameCheck is now a full item (see METH-211, arXiv 2611.26968).
