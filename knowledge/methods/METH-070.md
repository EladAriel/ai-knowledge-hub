# METH-070: Ramp: how to give AI context and build a company brain (playbook)

## Summary

Vendor post selling Ramp's Glass (knowledge base + connectors + owners), but the playbook is solid.

## Content

**4. [Ramp: How to give AI context and build a company brain](https://ramp.com/blog/how-to-give-ai-context)**
- **What it is:** a vendor post selling Ramp's Glass, and a rebrand of a knowledge base plus connectors plus owners.
- **The playbook is still solid:**
  - Write a 1–2 page context doc with definitions, policies, and where the truth lives for each kind of fact.
  - Set permissions before you connect anything.
  - Test with 10–20 questions you already know the answers to.
  - Give each section of the doc an owner.
  - Log every wrong answer as either a missing fact or an out-of-date fact.
- Adopt for:
  - *Architects:* write down where the truth lives as a source map, for example the CRM for customers and the ERP for finances.
  - *Product:* make the wrong-answer log a shared queue with owners.
  - *Devs:* put the 10–20 known-answer questions into CI from day one.
