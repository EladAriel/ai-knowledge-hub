# METH-035: Silent cue loss in document ingestion

## Summary

Converters drop highlight/colour marks on key requirements: F1 0.690 with formatting vs 0.150 after docling and 0.075 ….

## Details

76 policy docs; highlight and colour drive the gap, bold/italic barely matter; 32.8% of 6,460 docs use colour/highlight, 10.3% on obligations; tracked-change replacements not marked. ….
