## Content

### 2. [FAOPs: normal forms for skills libraries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22061) (JPMorgan Chase)
- **What is it:** A theory that refers to the skills library as a database. It includes:
  - Four normal forms: atomic decomposition, types, deduplication and least privileges.
  - Algebra for safe refactoring and parallel execution.
  - A four-level scale that classifies if AI is needed at all.
- **Why new:** It replaces the skill as a monolithic file with integrity between workflows, isolation of privileges and compilation before running.
- **Evidence:**
  - The formalism is complete, and there is a working example from the world of finance.
  - The claim that SkillBase runs in production and shortens development "from weeks to hours" is not supported by empirical research.
- **Scores:** Effectiveness 4/5 in regulatory environments, hype 3/5.
- **Fit:** High for corporate agents. **high** effort.
- **Recommendation: Pilot** for administration of the skills library. It is not advisable to adopt all the formalism at once.
