# METH-199: New terms (Nov 20–22, methods): Token fraud

## Summary

One-line mentions from the Nov 20–22 methods digests that did not become a full item (no full evaluations).

## Details

Token fraud: farms that use free trials of agent products to resell tokens. ChatPRD cancelled its trial because of this ([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/token-fraud-is-here-and-its-bad/)).

Other names from these three days became full items or merges: Harness evolution and AutoRAGBench (METH-196, METH-189), LoopRoute (METH-194), Inkling 2 Mini and looped transformer (METH-195), a harness adapter (METH-196), self-rewriting (merged into METH-081), prompt canary tokens and ZDR eligibility (METH-197), and SkillDiff / [FOCUS] (METH-198).

Filtered and therefore not added: Claude solving problems in black-hole physics, and Anthropic's share issuance, because they are news, not methods; a thread about a free Microsoft agents course, because it was lead-gen (Nov 20). Hype around "AI proved the Riemann hypothesis," because it is mathematics, not a product method; Toward a Science of AI Agent Societies, an opinion paper with no code; DriftGuard was already a full item the day before (Nov 21). Riemann-hypothesis proofs again; the Frostbite processor, which is not yet in silicon and whose vendor claims were not checked; Inkling 2 Mini at 5.4GB, already covered the day before; token fraud, already covered on Nov 20 (Nov 22).
