# METH-118: Gemini Connected Apps: human reviewers see Drive files and emails

## Summary

Google's help page confirms that human reviewers go over some Connected Apps data (Gmail, Drive, and related sources).

## Details

Recommendation: Adopt for controls. Privacy and data leaving agents is a recurring theme after late October and Nov 7. Source: Google help (support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988). The evidence, scores, fit and effort parts of the original note are not available; only the title, the opening and the Adopt recommendation are.
