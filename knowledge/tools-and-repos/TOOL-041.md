Three tools for structured answers and governed shared memory at the answer layer.

## Details

instructor (~14.1k★, MIT, pushed Nov 5): structured LLM output matching a Pydantic schema with retries across providers—borrow a fixed answer schema. caura (~550★, Apache-2.0, pushed Nov 6; formerly MemClaw): governed shared memory with visibility scopes/types and contradiction flags over MCP—borrow who-can-see-what; eToro case study is vendor marketing until verified. VeritasGraph (~326★, MIT): self-hosted GraphRAG that records provenance as facts are written and can decline without evidence—borrow answer paths that can say 'I don't know'.
